Version: 1.2.0
Date: 24. 10. 2026
  Features:
  - The transmitter can be wired into a circuit network and broadcasts its link status as virtual signals: spt-link-up (healthy link) and spt-link-failing (the upkeep is not being paid - the link drops within seconds); both signals absent means the link is down.
  - Circuit "Enable if" condition (a small panel next to the transmitter's window): while it is false the transmitter is off - no link, no upkeep drain.
  - The wire color carrying the status signals and the wire color watched by the condition are selectable (Both / Red / Green).
  - The condition and the wire choices transfer with the copy-paste tool and in blueprints.

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 18. 10. 2026
  Features:
  - Links a planet or space platform to the common space power grid: transfers energy through space over any distance, moving space platforms included.
  - Energy flows in both directions between all linked surfaces of one force and is rationed proportionally to demand when production falls short.
  - The transmitter draws a constant upkeep power to hold the link (600 MW by default; higher quality draws less); the link drops when the local network cannot supply it and is restored automatically once it can again.
  - Unlocked with Cryogenic science (researchable on Aquilo).

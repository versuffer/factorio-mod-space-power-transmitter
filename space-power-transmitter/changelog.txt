Version: 1.2.0
Date: 23. 10. 2026
  Features:
  - The transmitter can be wired into a circuit network and broadcasts its link status as virtual signals: spt-link-up (healthy link) and spt-link-failing (the local network stopped paying the upkeep - the link drops within seconds); both signals absent means the link is down.
  - Circuit "Enable if" condition (a small panel next to the transmitter's window, also available through the remote interface): while it is false the transmitter is off - no link, no upkeep drain, no link-lost alert. The condition transfers with the copy-paste tool and in blueprints.
  - The panel has a Wire selector (Both / Red / Green): it picks the wire color that carries the status signals and is watched by the Enable if condition.
  - The hidden status combinator sits offset from the transmitter, so it no longer blocks super force build (ctrl+shift+click) of transmitter blueprints over the existing entity.
  - The transmitter now has a circuit connector (the accumulator socket); the status signals ride on both wire colors via an invisible script-wired combinator.
  - The transmitter's own (meaningless) accumulator charge signal is suppressed on the circuit network.
  Bugfixes:
  - The status signal survived saving and loading: script wires and the combinator slot are rebuilt on session start (script wires are not serialized by the engine).

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 18. 10. 2026
  Features:
  - Links a planet or space platform to the common space power grid: transfers energy through space over any distance, moving space platforms included.
  - Energy flows in both directions between all linked surfaces of one force and is rationed proportionally to demand when production falls short.
  - The transmitter draws a constant upkeep power to hold the link (600 MW by default; higher quality draws less); the link drops when the local network cannot supply it and is restored automatically once it can again.
  - Unlocked with Cryogenic science (researchable on Aquilo).
